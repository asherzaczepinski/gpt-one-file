THE RUNPOD MODEL
================
A bigger version of the GPT from main_functionized.py, trained on a rented GPU for $0.93 on Nov 21, 2026.
Same forward pass, same hand-written backprop, same Adam. Just more numbers and way more text.


WHAT IT IS
----------
                          main_functionized.py (Mac)      RunPod model
  numbers per token       128                              256
  attention heads         4 (of 32 each)                   8 (of 32 each)
  blocks                  6                                6
  sees back               64 tokens                        128 tokens (about 100 words)
  learned numbers         2.4 million                      17.4 million  (17,389,588)
  tokens it knows         4,841                            24,596
  training text           stories.txt, 215,000 tokens      TinyStories, 97.5 million tokens (~450x more)
  windows per step        16                               32
  best test loss          3.48                             1.545

  (the two losses aren't a perfect apples-to-apples comparison, since they use different vocabularies,
   but the stories show the difference clearly, see below)

TinyStories = a free dataset of about 2 million short, simple children's stories, made for training small
models like this one. The run used the first 400 MB of it (382 MB after the download got cut at a story).


HOW IT WAS TRAINED
------------------
  GPU          NVIDIA RTX 2000 Ada (16 GB), RunPod secure cloud, $0.24/hour
  code         runpod/train_tinystories.py = main_functionized_cuda.py + a bigger data loader + a timed loop
  library      CuPy (numpy that runs on the GPU), float32
  speed        0.152 s per step = 26,944 tokens/sec (about 7x one Mac core)
  length       222 minutes, 87,619 steps, 359 million tokens = 3.7 trips through the text
  learning     Adam, learning rate 0.001, 200 warmup steps, cosine slide down to 10%, gradient clipping at 1
  test split   last 1% of the text, never trained on

The test loss, every ~9 minutes:
  start   10.153   (random guessing = 10.110)
   9 min   2.060
  18 min   1.920
  36 min   1.786
  62 min   1.708
  98 min   1.646
 125 min   1.612
 160 min   1.582
 196 min   1.553
 223 min   1.545   <- best, saved

It never started memorizing: the test loss kept going down the whole time and stayed BELOW the train loss
(1.575). That's the opposite of the Mac runs, where it started getting worse after about 6 minutes. More text
fixed it, just like the notes predicted.


WHAT IT WRITES
--------------
Same prompt, both models:

  Mac model (test loss 3.48):
    "Tom and Sam went to the park. They ran to slide and play again. They all played together and had fun
    on the slide. They felt good at Tom was happy and happy. Tim was not happy that he helped their play
    with the blocks."

  RunPod model (test loss 1.545):
    "Tom and Sam went to the park. They played on the swings and slide. They had fun. But soon, they got
    very tired. They needed to sat down on the grass. Tom said, "Sam, let's go home now. We need to rest."
    Sam agreed. They sat down under a big tree. ... They took a long nap. At the end of the day, they were
    both better."

Good at: grammar, dialogue with quote marks, keeping the same characters, stories with a start, a problem and
an ending, and the <|endoftext|> marker between stories.
Still bad at: logic sometimes ("Tom was too small to move his head off"), forgetting things from more than
~100 words back, and anything that isn't a simple kids' story. It has no facts and can't chat. Ask it a
question and it'll just write a story.

All 4 final stories: runpod/results/out/stories.txt


WHAT IT COST
------------
  balance before   $13.21
  balance after    $12.28
  total            $0.93   (+ about half a cent for a first try that crashed in a minute)

Spending limits in the launcher: it won't start unless the balance covers the budget + $0.25, it works out
how many minutes the budget buys and trains for that minus 15, the pod has its own timer that deletes it at
the limit (even if the Mac sleeps), and the launcher deletes the pod and checks it's gone at the end.


WHAT WENT WRONG ON THE WAY (all fixed in train_on_runpod.sh)
-----------------------------------------------------------
1. rest.runpod.io (RunPod's REST address) doesn't exist anymore, not even on public DNS.
   Fix: everything goes through the GraphQL API at api.runpod.io/graphql
   (podFindAndDeployOnDemand to create, pod(...) to find the ssh port, podTerminate to delete).
2. The pod's Ubuntu 24.04 blocked "pip install" (PEP 668, the same thing that happened on the Mac on day 1).
   Fix: pip install --break-system-packages (fine on a throwaway pod). The script now also stops right away
   if cupy can't use the GPU, instead of downloading data for nothing.
3. The ssh command that started training didn't come back until training finished, so the launcher froze
   and didn't print progress (training itself was fine).
   Fix: setsid + a 15-second cutoff on that ssh. (Not tested on a real pod yet.)


FILES
-----
  runpod/train_on_runpod.sh          the one-command launcher: rent, train, copy back, delete
  runpod/train_tinystories.py        the training code that runs on the pod
  runpod/results/out/best_model.pkl  the trained model (70 MB: params + the token list + settings)
  runpod/results/out/stories.txt     the 4 stories it wrote at the end
  runpod/results/train.log           everything the pod printed
  runpod/run_log.txt                 everything the launcher did, with times and balances
  runpod/write_story.py              write stories on the Mac with the trained model
  runpod/play.py + play.html         the playground (see below)
  lessons/runpod.html                how the launcher works, step by step


PLAY WITH IT
------------
  .venv/bin/python runpod/play.py
opens a page in your browser where you type the start of a story and watch it write, with sliders for
temperature, top-k, length and seed. Click any word it wrote to see the other tokens it was choosing between
and their chances. Ctrl+C in the terminal to stop it. Runs on the Mac's CPU, no GPU or money needed.

Or just print stories:
  .venv/bin/python runpod/write_story.py "Once upon a time, a dog"


RUN IT AGAIN
------------
  caffeinate -i bash runpod/train_on_runpod.sh                 about $1
  BUDGET=0.50 caffeinate -i bash runpod/train_on_runpod.sh     about 50 cents
To change the model size, edit the defaults at the top of train_tinystories.py before running, e.g.
  embedding_size = int(os.environ.get("EMBEDDING_SIZE", 256))   -> change 256 to 384
(typing EMBEDDING_SIZE=384 in front of the launcher command does NOT reach the pod). A new run overwrites runpod/results/, so copy the old
one somewhere first if you want to keep it.

Next steps that would make it smarter: train longer (the test loss was still slowly dropping), a bigger
model (512 numbers per token, 8 blocks), or seeing further back (256 tokens) so long stories stay on track.
