Embedding Map

Every token in my model has 256 learned numbers. Nobody told it what any word means, but after training, words used in similar ways ended up with similar numbers. This squashes those 256 numbers down to 3 so you can fly around them.

drag to spin · scroll or pinch to zoom · click a dot to see its neighbors

What you're looking at

Each dot is one token (a word or piece of a word) from my model trained on RunPod. Only tokens that show up at least 4 times in stories.txt are here, so the map isn't swamped by rare ones.

Neighborhoods (t-SNE) arranges the dots so tokens with similar numbers sit close together. It's great for spotting groups, like all the names, colors or animals bunching up, but the distances between faraway groups don't mean much.

Straight projection (PCA) just looks at the 256 numbers from the 3 directions where they vary the most. It's honest but crowded: those 3 directions only hold __EXPLAINED__% of the variation, because the model spreads meaning across all 256 numbers.

The neighbor list on the right doesn't use the map at all: it compares all 256 numbers directly (cosine similarity: 1 = pointing the exact same way, 0 = unrelated). That's the real answer for "what does the model think is similar".

Group colors are just word lists I picked to make patterns easy to see. The model never saw these labels. You can highlight up to 3 groups at once.
